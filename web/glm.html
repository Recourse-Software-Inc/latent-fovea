GLM-5.2, streamed from your SSD

744 billion parameters; about 40 billion take part in each token. The attention and shared parts stay on your GPU; the 19,200 routed experts stay on your drive and are read as the router asks for them.

Set up

Pick the folder holding colibri's GLM-5.2 container (mastouri/GLM-5.2-colibri-int4-g64-with-int8-mtp, ~419 GB), for example on an external SSD. Nothing is copied: the browser reads the bytes it needs, where they are. Your browser asks once for permission to read the folder.
